What is this?

Every fourth-year engineering student at Carleton completes a capstone project. This system brings the whole process into one place: professors post project topics, students browse topics and apply to the ones that fit their program, and the project coordinator tracks which students still need a project and organizes oral presentations and final report deadlines.

Who uses it?

Students

Browse available project topics, filter by program, and submit your availability for oral presentations.

How it works

  1. Professors post project topics.
  2. Students browse topics and apply to one that matches their program.
  3. The coordinator follows up with students who have no project.
  4. Students submit availability, and oral presentations are scheduled.
  5. Teams submit their final reports before the coordinator's deadline.
